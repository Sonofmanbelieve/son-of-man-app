SON OF MAN APP V4 — SMART BOOKING STAGE
========================================
This prototype now demonstrates:
- Quantity-based laundry pricing: ₦1,000 × clothes.
- Quantity-based duvet pricing: ₦3,500 × duvets.
- Service date, time, address, phone and instructions.
- Pickup/delivery option.
- Corporate/large-job quote workflow.
- Premium quotation request screen.
- Flexible team capacity messaging for bulk jobs.
- Laundry turnaround message: 3–4 days, subject to weather/drying conditions.
